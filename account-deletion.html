🐰 Tokki - Account & Data Deletion Request

App Name: Tokki - AI Companion

Last Updated: April 28, 2026

📌 How to Delete Your Account

Option 1. In-App Deletion (Recommended)
1Open Tokki app → Go to Settings
2Select Delete Account
3Follow the confirmation steps to complete
Option 2. Request via Email

If you cannot access the app, you can request deletion via email.

📧 Email: privacy@bitterpill.chat

Please include:

  • Subject: [Tokki] Account Deletion Request
  • Your registered email address (Google account email) for verification
  • Reason for deletion (optional)

Requests are processed within 7 business days.

🗑️ Data That Will Be Deleted

Data TypeActionRetention
Account info (email, user ID)Immediately deletedNone
Nickname & profile infoImmediately deletedNone
Chat messages & historyImmediately deletedNone
Subscription/payment recordsAnonymized & retained5 years (legal req.)
Access & security logsAnonymized & retained3 months (legal req.)

Note: Some data is retained as required by law and will be automatically deleted after the legally required retention period ends.

⚠️ Important Notes

  • Account deletion permanently removes all conversation history and cannot be undone.
  • Active subscriptions must be canceled separately through Google Play.
  • You will receive a confirmation email once your deletion request is processed.
